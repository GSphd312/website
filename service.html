Service

School of Public Health Representative for Graduate Student Advisory Board of WIM (Workshop in Methods)

The main job is to help facilitate introductory training in sophisticated research methods to faculty and graduate students at Indiana University-Bloomington. One of the goals is to supplement statistics courses across schools with diversified workshops provided by scholars from Indiana University and across the US.
